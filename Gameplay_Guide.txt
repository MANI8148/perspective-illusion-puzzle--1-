================================================================================
                    PERSPECTIVE ILLUSION PUZZLE
                         GAMEPLAY GUIDE
================================================================================

WELCOME TO THE ILLUSION
Navigate impossible geometry using the power of perspective! In this game, 
you must guide your character through 12 mind-bending levels where optical 
illusions become reality. If two disconnected platforms look like they connect 
from your camera angle, they DO connect. 

================================================================================
1. CORE MECHANICS
================================================================================
* MOVEMENT: Click on any adjacent platform to walk to it.
* PERSPECTIVE JUMPS: Rotate the camera until two disconnected platforms 
  overlap perfectly on your screen. When they align, they will glow GOLD. 
  You can now click across the gap as if they were physically connected!
* ROTATING BLOCKS: Some levels feature platforms that rotate over time. 
  Time your movements and camera angles to cross them.
* FIRST-PERSON MODE: Press 'V' to enter an immersive first-person view. 
  Experience the impossible geometry from the ground level!

================================================================================
2. CONTROLS
================================================================================
[Mouse]
* Left-Click & Drag : Orbit the camera around the level
* Scroll Wheel      : Zoom in / Zoom out
* Left-Click        : Move the player to the targeted platform

[Keyboard]
* V                 : Toggle First-Person Camera Mode
* R                 : Restart the current level
* Esc               : Return to the Main Menu
* W/A/S/D or Arrows : Move while in First-Person Mode

================================================================================
3. PLATFORM TYPES & HAZARDS
================================================================================
* Start Node        : Blue glowing beacon. This is where you begin the level.
* Goal Node         : Green glowing runes. Reach this to complete the level.
* Aligned Node      : Gold glow. Indicates a successful perspective illusion.
* Standard Pillar   : Safe platform you can walk on.
* Obstacles         : Avoid getting too close! Taking damage reduces your 
                      health (maximum 3 hearts). Lose all health and you will 
                      die and have to respawn.

================================================================================
4. LEVEL PROGRESSION & HINTS
================================================================================
Level 1  - Basics: Click adjacent platforms to walk.
Level 2  - The Illusion: Rotate camera until platforms overlap.
Level 3  - Spiral: Follow the spiral staircase upwards.
Level 4  - Leap: A leap of faith — align from afar.
Level 5  - Palace: Multi-floor palace — climb and bridge.
Level 6  - Clockwork: Sync with the clockwork rotation.
Level 7  - Double Jump: Two illusion jumps required.
Level 8  - Bridge: Ascend stairs, cross the rotating bridge.
Level 9  - The Chain: Chain three illusions to escape.
Level 10 - Crystal Maze: Mind the altitude gap.
Level 11 - Garden: Float across two garden terraces.
Level 12 - Ancient Temple: Find the hidden altar.
